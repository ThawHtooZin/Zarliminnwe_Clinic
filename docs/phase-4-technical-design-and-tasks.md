# Phase 4 Technical Design and Task Breakdown

Currently Planning: Phase 4 - Ultra-Minimal Patient And Finance Module

## Clinic Management System - Phase 4 Ultra-Minimal Patient And Finance Module

### Purpose

Phase 4 adds only the ultra-minimal patient visit and finance features defined in the PRD.

The focus is:

- Ultra-minimal patient visit records.
- Service income linked to patient visit records.
- General income tracking.
- Expense tracking.
- Income, expense, and net balance summaries.

Phase 4 must not add EHR, clinical records, prescriptions, vitals, diagnosis, medical notes, appointment booking, doctor scheduling, queues, or appointment records.

This document is for technical design and Epic breakdown only. No application code is generated by this document.

### Source Inputs

- PRD: `docs/clinic-management-system-prd.md`

- Phase 2 design: `docs/phase-2-technical-design-and-tasks.md`

- Existing expected modules:
  - Auth and roles.

  - Unit management.
  - Stock ledger and balances.
  - Pharmacy sales.
  - Stock control reports.
  - Audit logs.

## 1. Strict Phase 4 Scope

### 1.1 Included

Phase 4 includes only:

- Patient visit records with name, age, and visit datetime.
- Create, view, edit, and list patient visit records.
- Service income linked directly to one patient visit when a patient fee is collected.
- Service income without a patient link for non-patient service income.
- General income categories.
- Expense categories.
- Expense records.
- Date-range financial summaries.
- Filters for finance records.
- Basic audit logs for patient visit and finance changes.

### 1.2 Explicitly Excluded

Phase 4 must not include:

- Clinical history.
- Symptoms.
- Diagnosis.
- Treatment plans.
- Prescriptions as clinical records.
- Vitals.
- Lab results.
- Medical notes.
- Doctor notes.
- Attachments or clinical documents.
- Appointment booking.
- Appointment status.
- Doctor scheduling.
- Queue management.
- Patient portal.
- Insurance claims.
- Complex billing, invoices, credit, debt, or account receivable workflows.
- Any dependency from pharmacy sales, stock, products, or units into the patient module.

### 1.3 Boundary Rules

- Patient visit records are for service fee reference only.
- Patient visit records do not control pharmacy sales.
- Pharmacy POS must continue to work without patient selection.
- Finance records must not post stock ledger rows.
- Finance records must not change product stock, product units, purchase receipts, or sale lines.
- Pharmacy sales income remains separate from service income source records.
- Financial summaries may show pharmacy sales totals as a separate read-only source.

## 2. Technical Design

### 2.1 Architecture Goals

- Keep patient visit logic independent from pharmacy sales, stock, and unit management.
- Keep finance logic independent from stock movements.
- Make patient records intentionally small and hard to extend accidentally into EHR.
- Keep service income traceable to patient visit when applicable.
- Keep pharmacy sales income and service income separate at source.
- Provide simple date-range finance summaries without building a full accounting system.
- Use existing auth, roles, and audit patterns.
- Keep every table in its own dedicated migration file.

### 2.2 Proposed Domain Modules

#### Patient Visits

Owns ultra-minimal patient visit records.

Responsibilities:

- Create a visit record.
- Edit only name, age, and visit datetime.
- List and search visit records.
- Expose visit records for service income linking.
- Prevent clinical or appointment fields from entering the module.

Suggested namespace:

- `App\Domain\Patients`

#### Finance

Owns service income, general income, expenses, and financial summaries.

Responsibilities:

- Manage income categories.
- Manage expense categories.
- Record income entries.
- Link income entries to patient visits when applicable.
- Record expenses.
- Summarize income, expenses, pharmacy sales totals, and net balance by date range.
- Keep finance source records separate from pharmacy sale records.

Suggested namespace:

- `App\Domain\Finance`

## 3. Core Data Model

### 3.1 `patient_visits`

Stores ultra-minimal patient visit records.

Key fields:

- `id`
- `patient_name`
- `age`
- `visited_at`
- `created_by`
- timestamps

Rules:

- `patient_name` is required.
- `age` is required and must be a valid non-negative whole number.
- `visited_at` is required.
- `created_by` references the user who created the record.
- Only name, age, and visit datetime are patient visit business fields.
- Do not add phone, address, gender, symptoms, diagnosis, vitals, notes, prescription, doctor, department, queue, or appointment fields.
- Records can be edited for correction, but edits should be audited.

### 3.2 `income_categories`

Stores allowed income categories for non-pharmacy income.

Key fields:

- `id`
- `name`
- `type`
- `description`
- `is_active`
- timestamps

Type values:

- `service`
- `general`

Rules:

- Category name must be unique.
- Service income categories can be linked to patient visits.
- General income categories do not require patient visits.
- Inactive categories remain available for historical records but cannot be selected for new entries.

Suggested default categories:

- Consultation Fee.
- Service Fee.
- Other Income.

### 3.3 `expense_categories`

Stores expense categories.

Key fields:

- `id`
- `name`
- `description`
- `is_active`
- timestamps

Rules:

- Category name must be unique.
- Inactive categories remain available for historical records but cannot be selected for new expenses.

Suggested default categories:

- Rent.
- Salary.
- Utilities.
- Supplies.
- Maintenance.
- Other Expense.

### 3.4 `income_entries`

Stores service income and general income outside pharmacy sales.

Key fields:

- `id`
- `income_category_id`
- `patient_visit_id`
- `amount`
- `payment_method`
- `received_at`
- `received_by`
- `description`
- timestamps

Payment method values:

- `cash`
- `card`
- `mobile`
- `bank_transfer`
- `other`

Rules:

- `income_category_id` is required.
- `patient_visit_id` is nullable.
- `patient_visit_id` links directly to one patient visit when a patient fee is collected.
- `amount` must be greater than zero.
- `received_at` is required.
- `received_by` references the collecting user.
- Pharmacy sales must not be copied into `income_entries`.
- Financial summaries may read pharmacy sales separately from the `sales` table.
- Existing entries should not be silently deleted after reporting; corrections should use controlled edit permissions and audit logs.

### 3.5 `expense_entries`

Stores general clinic or pharmacy expenses.

Key fields:

- `id`
- `expense_category_id`
- `amount`
- `expense_date`
- `payee`
- `payment_method`
- `description`
- `created_by`
- timestamps

Payment method values:

- `cash`
- `card`
- `mobile`
- `bank_transfer`
- `other`

Rules:

- `expense_category_id` is required.
- `amount` must be greater than zero.
- `expense_date` is required.
- `payee` is optional but recommended.
- `description` is optional.
- Expenses do not affect inventory.
- Expenses do not create stock ledger rows.

## 4. Service Design

### 4.1 `PatientVisitService`

Responsibilities:

- Create patient visit records.
- Update only allowed patient visit fields.
- Search visits by patient name and visit date range.
- Provide patient visit options for income entry linking.
- Reject any clinical or appointment-like payload fields.
- Write audit logs for create and update actions.

Important rule:

- This service must not call stock, product, unit, sale checkout, or appointment logic.

### 4.2 `IncomeEntryService`

Responsibilities:

- Create service and general income entries.
- Validate income category status.
- Validate optional patient visit link.
- Save received amount, method, datetime, and collected user.
- List and filter income entries.
- Write audit logs for create and update actions.

Important rule:

- This service stores service and general income only. It must not duplicate pharmacy sale totals into income entries.

### 4.3 `ExpenseEntryService`

Responsibilities:

- Create expense entries.
- Validate expense category status.
- Save amount, date, payee, payment method, description, and user.
- List and filter expense entries.
- Write audit logs for create and update actions.

Important rule:

- This service must not interact with inventory or stock ledger services.

### 4.4 `FinanceSummaryService`

Responsibilities:

- Summarize `income_entries` by date range.
- Summarize `expense_entries` by date range.
- Read completed non-voided pharmacy sales totals as a separate source when needed.
- Return service income, general income, pharmacy sales income, expenses, and net balance.
- Support category and payment method filters where practical.

Net balance rule:

- Net balance equals service/general income plus completed pharmacy sales income minus expenses.
- Voided pharmacy sales must be excluded.
- Pharmacy sales must be labeled separately from service/general income.

## 5. User Flows

### 5.1 Patient Visit Creation

1. User opens patient visit create screen.
2. User enters patient name, age, and visit datetime.
3. System validates only those patient visit business fields.
4. System creates patient visit.
5. System logs audit event.
6. User can link the visit to a service income entry if a fee is collected.

### 5.2 Service Income With Patient Visit

1. User opens income entry create screen.
2. User selects service income category.
3. User optionally selects a patient visit.
4. User enters amount, payment method, received datetime, and description if needed.
5. System validates amount and patient visit link.
6. System saves income entry.
7. System logs audit event.

### 5.3 Service Or General Income Without Patient Visit

1. User opens income entry create screen.
2. User selects service or general income category.
3. User leaves patient visit empty.
4. User enters amount, payment method, received datetime, and description if needed.
5. System saves income entry.
6. System logs audit event.

### 5.4 Expense Entry

1. User opens expense entry create screen.
2. User selects expense category.
3. User enters amount, expense date, payee, payment method, and description if needed.
4. System saves expense entry.
5. System logs audit event.

### 5.5 Finance Summary

1. User opens finance summary report.
2. User selects date range.
3. System reads income entries.
4. System reads expense entries.
5. System reads completed non-voided pharmacy sales totals as a separate source.
6. System shows service/general income, pharmacy sales income, expenses, and net balance.

- Amount is required.

## 12. Key Risks And Decisions

### Risk: Patient module grows into EHR

Decision:

- Store only patient name, age, and visit datetime as patient visit business fields.
- Reject clinical and appointment-like request fields.
- Keep UI free of clinical and appointment labels.
- Add tests proving clinical fields cannot be stored.

### Risk: Patient records become required for pharmacy sales

Decision:

- Patient visits remain independent.
- Pharmacy sales continue to work without patient selection.
- Finance patient link is optional.
- No patient logic is added to stock posting.

### Risk: Pharmacy sales are duplicated into finance income entries

Decision:

- Pharmacy sales remain in `sales`.
- Finance summary reads completed sale totals separately.
- Income entries store only service and general income.

### Risk: Finance module becomes full accounting

Decision:

- Phase 4 supports simple income, expense, and net summaries only.
- No invoices, accounts receivable, debt tracking, ledgers, journal entries, tax accounting, or insurance claims.

### Risk: Expenses accidentally affect inventory

Decision:

- Expense entries do not call stock services.
- Expenses do not create stock ledger rows.
- Stock changes remain inside inventory workflows only.
